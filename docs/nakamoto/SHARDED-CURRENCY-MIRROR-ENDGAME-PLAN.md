## The disease (one disease, two faces)

Across ~22 of ~24 distinct currency-mirror failures (#123 → run-28), one root recurs: gl0/the producer depends on **reconstructing** per-MG currency state from a locally-complete chain-linked window of raw binaries, and different actors read **different versions of S(N)**. The repeatedly-named cure (VERSION-MODEL §4) is to anchor the **three references of the prior S(N)** — producer window-anchor (`perMgTip`), producer diff-prior, gl0 apply-prior — at the **same finalized base**, with the window covering base→latest (re-including adopted-but-unfinalized binaries) and progress gated on **finalization, not adoption**.
